EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================

This report provides a comprehensive analysis of the sidelink dataframe from the Berlin 
V2X experiment. The dataset contains 325,868 records with 70 columns covering V2V 
communication parameters, GPS coordinates, vehicle telemetry, weather data, and traffic 
information. This dataset represents the most complete V2V communication dataset in the 
Berlin V2X collection, with 100% data completeness and excellent SUMO visualization potential.

DATASET OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Total Records: 325,868 measurements
Total Columns: 70 data fields
Data Completeness: 100.0% (no missing values)
Time Period: Timestamp issues detected (1970 epoch)
GPS Coverage: 100.0% (325,868 valid GPS points)

Device Distribution:
- Source Devices: 1, 2, 3, 4 (balanced distribution)
- Destination Devices: 1, 2, 3, 4 (balanced distribution)
- Scenario Distribution: S1 (169,793 records), S2 (156,075 records)

   - MCS (Modulation and Coding Scheme): 9.916 ± 1.998
   - NOISE POWER: 0.007 ± 0.051

   - Rx_power: 0.533 ± 8.824

2. GPS AND LOCATION DATA

CONCLUSION
================================================================================

The sidelink dataframe is the most complete V2V communication dataset in the Berlin V2X 
collection, providing comprehensive insights into V2V communication scenarios. Despite 
timestamp issues, it offers excellent potential for SUMO visualization due to its 100% 
GPS completeness, complete V2V communication parameters, and comprehensive data coverage.

The dataset's strength lies in its complete V2V communication coverage, making it ideal 
for creating realistic SUMO simulations that incorporate V2V communication, vehicle 
positioning, and environmental conditions. When combined with other Berlin V2X datasets, 
it provides a complete picture of V2X communication performance in real-world scenarios.

The 100% data completeness and GPS coverage make it particularly suitable for SUMO 
visualization, enabling realistic V2V communication simulation with accurate vehicle 
positioning and signal quality mapping in real-world Berlin scenarios.
