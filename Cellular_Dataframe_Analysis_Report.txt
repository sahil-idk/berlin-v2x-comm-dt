================================================================================
CELLULAR DATAFRAME ANALYSIS REPORT
================================================================================

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
================================================================================

This report provides a comprehensive analysis of the cellular dataframe from the Berlin 
V2X experiment. The dataset contains 207,434 records with 163 columns covering cellular 
network performance, vehicle telemetry, weather data, and traffic information. This is 
the most comprehensive dataset in the Berlin V2X collection, providing multi-dimensional 
insights into V2X communication scenarios with excellent SUMO visualization potential.

DATASET OVERVIEW
================================================================================

Total Records: 207,434 measurements
Total Columns: 163 data fields
Data Completeness: 48.9% (significant missing data)
Time Period: June 22-24, 2021 (2 days, 9 hours, 10 minutes)
GPS Coverage: 98.8% (204,942 valid GPS points)

Device Distribution:
- pc3: 60,422 records (29.1%)
- pc1: 60,208 records (29.0%)
- pc2: 43,458 records (21.0%)
- pc4: 43,346 records (20.9%)

WHAT THE DATASET PROVIDES
================================================================================

1. GPS AND LOCATION DATA
   - GPS Coordinates: Latitude (52.469215 to 52.517725), Longitude (13.236470 to 13.377518)
   - Altitude: Vehicle elevation data
   - Course Over Ground (COG): Vehicle direction
   - Speed: Vehicle speed in km/h
   - High GPS completeness: 98.8% of records have valid coordinates

2. CELLULAR NETWORK PERFORMANCE
   - Primary Cell (PCell) measurements: 126 cellular network columns
   - RSRP (Reference Signal Received Power): -90.799 ± 12.034 dBm
   - RSRQ (Reference Signal Received Quality): -11.385 ± 3.141 dB
   - RSSI (Received Signal Strength Indicator): Available
   - SNR (Signal-to-Noise Ratio): Available
   - MCS (Modulation and Coding Scheme): Available
   - Frequency and bandwidth information
   - Cell identification and network parameters

3. NETWORK PERFORMANCE METRICS
   - Datarate: 21.2 Mbps ± 29.5 Mbps (189,596 measurements)
   - Jitter: 4,121 ms ± 324,721 ms (189,596 measurements)
   - Ping: 470 ms ± 1,418 ms (147,471 measurements)
   - Target datarate: 134 Mbps ± 150 Mbps

4. VEHICLE TELEMETRY DATA
   - Speed: Vehicle speed measurements
   - Course Over Ground: Vehicle direction
   - Altitude: Vehicle elevation
   - Wind speed: Environmental wind conditions
   - Direction: Vehicle movement direction

5. WEATHER AND ENVIRONMENTAL DATA
   - Temperature: Environmental temperature
   - Humidity: Atmospheric humidity
   - Pressure: Atmospheric pressure
   - Wind: Wind speed and conditions
   - Precipitation: Weather conditions
   - Visibility: Environmental visibility

6. TRAFFIC AND ROAD CONDITIONS
   - Traffic Jam Factor: Real-time traffic congestion
   - Traffic Street Name: Road identification
   - Traffic Distance: Distance measurements
   - Road condition information

7. TEMPORAL INFORMATION
   - Timestamps: Precise timing data
   - Duration: 2 days, 9 hours, 10 minutes
   - Time range: June 22-24, 2021
   - Chronological ordering available

WHAT THE DATASET LACKS
================================================================================

1. DATA COMPLETENESS ISSUES
   - 48.9% overall data completeness
   - Significant missing values in many columns
   - Inconsistent data availability across measurements
   - Potential data quality issues

2. V2X SPECIFIC DATA
   - No direct V2V communication measurements
   - No sidelink or D2D communication data
   - Limited V2I communication context
   - No vehicle-to-vehicle interaction data

3. DETAILED NETWORK ANALYSIS
   - No packet-level analysis
   - No protocol-specific measurements
   - Limited network topology information
   - No handover or mobility data

4. CONTEXTUAL INFORMATION
   - No test scenario descriptions
   - No network configuration details
   - No measurement methodology information
   - Limited quality of service parameters

CONCLUSION
================================================================================

The cellular dataframe is the most comprehensive dataset in the Berlin V2X collection,
providing multi-dimensional insights into V2X communication scenarios. Despite data
completeness issues, it offers excellent potential for SUMO visualization due to its
GPS coordinates, vehicle telemetry, and environmental data. The dataset is particularly
valuable for real-world V2X simulation, network performance visualization, and
environmental impact analysis.

The dataset's strength lies in its comprehensive coverage of V2X communication factors,
making it ideal for creating realistic SUMO simulations that incorporate vehicle
movement, network performance, and environmental conditions. When combined with other
Berlin V2X datasets, it provides a complete picture of V2X communication performance
in real-world scenarios.

The high GPS completeness (98.8%) and temporal data make it particularly suitable for
SUMO visualization, enabling realistic vehicle tracking and communication quality
mapping in real-world Berlin scenarios.
